### SpaceGame

Blast off into SpaceGame! 🚀✨
In this fast-paced, space-themed arcade game, you’ll shoot your way through waves of rogue asteroids, collect out-of-this-world power-ups, and dodge cosmic chaos—all while having a blast! Power up your ship, avoid becoming space dust, and aim for a high score that’s truly stellar. It’s time to rocket through the galaxy, one asteroid at a time.

So strap in, star commander—because in SpaceGame, the only thing more infinite than space... is the fun! 🌌💥

Warning: May cause extreme gravitational pull toward endless gameplay.
